## 1. Overview & Core Concept

- **What It Does**: Transforms the template browsing experience from a single generic gallery into an interconnected UK SEO ecosystem. A central `/cv-templates` hub showcases 4 flagship general/classic layouts at the top, provides interactive filtering across the full library, and funnels searchers into 7 high-intent UK category landing pages—each featuring the exact target template count, zoomable A4 previews populated with realistic UK candidate profiles, recruiter-written British formatting guidance, and tailored FAQs.
- **Target Audience / Persona**: UK jobseekers across distinct career stages—from 16-year-olds writing a first Saturday-job CV with GCSEs, school leavers applying for degree apprenticeships, and university graduates, to Spotlight actors, City of London finance professionals, NMC-registered NHS nurses, and corporate applicants navigating strict ATS filters.
- **Key Value**: Matches exact UK search intent with immediate visual proof. Instead of showing a senior corporate finance sample CV to a 16-year-old student or an actor, each landing page proves on first glance that the builder understands British hiring conventions for their exact field.

## 2. User Experience & Visual Design

### Key User Flows

1. **Organic Search Arrival on a Niche Spoke (e.g., `/cv-template-16-year-old` or `/acting-cv-template`)**:
   - **Above the Fold**: Visitor lands on a crisp, keyword-aligned hero with breadcrumb navigation (`Home / CV Templates / 16-Year-Old CV Templates`), clear British value proposition, and immediate visibility into the curated A4 template cards (2, 3, or 4 depending on the category) rendered live with role-authentic UK sample data.
   - **Inspect & Compare**: Clicking any A4 card or **Full Preview** opens the interactive zoom modal (`Fit`, `100%`, zoom +/-) displaying the niche-tailored British sample CV (e.g., GCSE subject breakdown for teens, stage/screen credit columns for actors, NMC clinical governance for nurses).
   - **Instant Builder Launch**: Clicking **Use Template** sets the chosen template in persistent builder state and routes directly to `/builder`, ready to edit and export as a 300 DPI A4 PDF or editable Word (`.docx`) document.
   - **Editorial Guidance & Cross-Discovery**: Scrolling below the templates reveals concise, actionable British recruiter rules for that category (e.g., UK child employment / National Insurance rules for 16-year-olds, Spotlight headshot rules for UK actors vs. Equality Act photo-free norms for corporate roles), an interactive FAQ accordion, and internal links to sibling CV template categories.
2. **Browsing the Canonical `/cv-templates` Hub**:
   - Leads with the **4 Verified General / Classic Flagship Templates** above the fold so broad `free cv templates` searchers can launch immediately.
   - Follows with an asymmetric **Browse by Career Stage & Industry** spoke navigator linking cleanly to all 7 dedicated URLs, plus interactive category filter tabs to explore every template in the full studio library.

### Visual Identity & Theme

- **Aesthetic Direction**: Authoritative British editorial clarity harmonized with the existing `freecvmakeruk.co.uk` visual language—combining deep midnight-navy hero anchors, crisp slate-white reading canvases, and pixel-accurate ISO A4 live paper previews.
- **Color Palette & 60-30-10 Balance**:
  - *60% Dominant Neutral Canvas*: Clean cool off-white (`#F8FAFC`) and pure white (`#FFFFFF`) document surfaces for effortless reading and high A4 paper contrast.
  - *30% Structural Surfaces*: Deep royal navy (`#090D1F` to `#0F1B4D`) hero headers, subtle slate hairline borders (`#E2E8F0`), and high-legibility slate ink (`#0F172A` headings, `#334155` prose).
  - *10% Accent Budget*: Brand cobalt/indigo (`#2563EB` / `#4B4DED`) reserved strictly for primary **Use Template** CTAs, active filter states, and interactive focus rings, paired with emerald (`#059669`) verification checkmarks.
- **Typography & Hierarchy**:
  - *Display Headings*: `Plus Jakarta Sans` (`clamp(2rem, 3.8vw, 3.25rem)`, tight tracking, `text-wrap: balance`) to prevent orphan trailing words.
  - *Body & Editorial Guides*: Clean readable prose (`15px–16px`, `1.65` line-height, constrained `65ch–72ch` measure for writing guides) with `tabular-nums` on template counts, dates, and A4 dimensions.
  - *Zero-Pill Metadata Discipline*: Card metadata (layout architecture, photo support, A4 dimensions, ideal-for summaries) renders as quiet, unboxed inline typography separated by middle dots (`·`) rather than cluttered multi-badge pill stacks. Interactive category filters remain clean, single-line segmented buttons (`whitespace-nowrap shrink-0`).
- **Section Rhythm (`Proposition → Live Templates → Niche Proof & Recruiter Rules → FAQ & Conversion`)**:
  - Every page follows a disciplined 4-part narrative arc without decorative fluff, fake scorecards, or thick left callout borders.
- **Interactive Feedback & Motion**:
  - Live `ResizeObserver` scaling keeps every 794×1123px A4 preview razor-sharp across 1440px desktop monitors and 375px mobile viewports.
  - GPU-composited hover elevation (`transform`, `opacity` within `150–200ms`) on template cards, accessible keyboard `Escape` handling on preview modals, and smooth accordion transitions.

## 3. Key Product Decisions & Trade-Offs

- **Decision 1: Permanent Canonical Consolidation (`/templates` → `/cv-templates`)**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Configure a permanent `308` redirect from `/templates` to `/cv-templates` and update all internal navigation (`TopNav`, `Footer`, Home showcase, Blog CTAs, and `sitemap.ts`) to point directly to `/cv-templates`.
  - *Why*: Eliminates duplicate-content cannibalization for *free cv templates* while preserving bookmarks or external links hitting `/templates`.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Keeping both `/templates` and `/cv-templates` live—rejected because two near-identical hubs dilute PageRank and confuse Google's canonical selection.

- **Decision 2: Purpose-Built Niche A4 Templates Registered Globally**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Create dedicated, print-calibrated A4 template components for specialized niches (**West End / Spotlight Acting**, **First Steps 16-Year-Old GCSE**, and **Pathway School Leaver & Apprenticeship**) and register them in the central CV Template Registry alongside existing templates (`London`, `Westminster`, `Oxford`, `Hopkins`, `Cambridge`, `Cardiff`, `Leeds`, `Belfast`, `Glasgow`, `Toronto`).
  - *Why*: Registering niche templates as first-class citizens guarantees that when a user clicks **Use Template** on `/acting-cv-template` or `/cv-template-16-year-old`, the exact layout they previewed works seamlessly inside `/builder`, template switching, share links, and 300 DPI PDF / DOCX export.
  - *Alternatives Considered*: Static mockup previews on landing pages—rejected because users expect the builder to render the exact layout promised on the landing page.

- **Decision 3: Role-Specific Sample CVs Decoupled from User Builder Data**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Render landing page cards and zoom modals using rich, niche-specific UK sample CV datasets, while clicking **Use Template** updates only the active `templateId` (and sensible default photo visibility, e.g., enabled on Acting where headshots are mandatory, disabled on UK corporate/ATS pages per Equality Act 2010 norms) before routing directly to `/builder`. Also provide a secondary optional button inside the preview modal (*"Edit with this UK example"*) in case a first-time teen or actor wants to start from the sample text rather than a blank form.
  - *Why*: Protects returning users from accidentally overwriting their typed CV details when switching templates, while still giving blank-slate beginners an optional one-click starter text path.

- **Decision 4: Full Technical SEO & Rich Snippet Schema Per URL**
  - *Chosen Approach*: Every hub and spoke page receives bespoke server-rendered `<title>` (35–60 chars), `<meta name="description">` (130–158 chars), canonical URL, OpenGraph/Twitter summary cards, and combined Schema.org `@graph` JSON-LD (`CollectionPage` + `ItemList` of templates + `BreadcrumbList` + category-specific `FAQPage`).
  - *Why*: Maximises click-through rates and rich-result eligibility in Google UK (`en-GB`) SERPs for both verified high-volume head terms and long-tail niche queries.

### Data Model & State

- **SEO Category Registry**: Strongly typed configuration mapping each of the 8 slugs (`cv-templates` + 7 niche slugs) to:
  - Target keyword metadata (`title`, `metaDescription`, `h1`, `subtitle`, `canonicalSlug`, `breadcrumbLabel`).
  - Curated list of exact `templateIds` matching the verified counts (`4`, `3`, `2`, `3`, `2`, `2`, `2`, `2`).
  - Dedicated `sampleCv: CV` profile tailored to UK standards for that exact demographic/profession.
  - Structured editorial sections: UK Recruiter Checklist (3–4 concrete formatting rules, e.g., GCSE grading 9–1 for teens, NMC PIN placement for nurses, Spotlight physical/credits format for actors), key section order breakdown, and 4–5 niche-specific FAQs.
- **Expanded Template Registry**:
  - Adds dedicated A4 layouts for **Spotlight / West End Acting** (structured credits list, performance attributes bar, training & skills), **First-Job 16-Year-Old** (skills & GCSE qualifications upfront, school responsibilities, volunteering/part-time experience), and **School Leaver / Apprenticeship** (A-Level/BTEC/T-Level focus, personal ambition, transferable project achievements) so every niche has authentic visual differentiation while remaining 100% compatible with the `CV` data schema and multi-page pagination engine.
- **Builder Query & Store Synchronization**:
  - Selecting a template invokes `useCvStore.getState().setTemplate(templateId)` and navigates to `/builder?template=<templateId>`.
  - `/builder` also reads `?template=<id>` on mount (if valid in the registry) so direct deep links or shared template links always open the exact requested layout.

### Interactive Component & State Mapping

- **Template Showcase Cards**:
  - *Click Preview / Card Thumbnail* → Opens `TemplatePreviewModal` hydrated with the category's custom British `sampleCv` so zoomed inspection reflects realistic niche content.
  - *Click "Use Template"* → Updates `activeTemplate` in Zustand store (plus toggles `showPhoto` appropriately when a template explicitly centers on headshots, such as Acting) and routes immediately to `/builder`.
- **Zoom Preview Modal**:
  - *Zoom Controls (`-`, `+`, `Fit`, `100%`)* → Dynamically scales the 794×1123px A4 sheet cleanly on mobile and desktop.
  - *Primary CTA ("Use Template")* → Applies template and opens `/builder` with the user's current CV data intact.
  - *Secondary Action ("Start with this UK example" on niche pages)* → Loads both the selected template and the niche sample CV into the builder store for users who want pre-written UK placeholder phrasing to edit.
- **Hub Filter & Spoke Navigator (`/cv-templates`)**:
  - *Top Flagship Grid* → Displays the 4 verified General / Classic templates immediately.
  - *Spoke Cards* → Navigates to each of the 7 specialized category URLs with clear template counts and audience summaries.
  - *Interactive Filter Bar* → Filters the complete studio template catalog client-side with zero page reload.
- **Global Navigation, Sitemap & Redirects**:
  - `next.config.ts` permanently redirects `/templates` → `/cv-templates`.
  - `TopNav`, `Footer` (expanded with direct links to key category spokes for strong internal crawl depth), `TemplatesSection` on the Home page, and `app/sitemap.ts` all reference `/cv-templates` and the 7 spoke URLs with appropriate priorities (`0.95` hub, `0.90` verified spokes).
